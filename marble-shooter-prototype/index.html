Marble shooter

Scarab Coil

Marbles roll toward the hole. Tap where you want to shoot and match 3 or more of a colour to pop them.

  • Glowing marbles hold powers: slow-down, reverse or bomb.
  • Hit in a row to build a streak bonus.
  • Tap the scarab to swap your marble.
  • The temple fights back: curses and blessings strike mid-level. Watch for the warning.
