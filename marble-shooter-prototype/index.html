A cozy virus-busting shooter

Cozy Cure

A chain of viruses is creeping toward your patient's heart. Send out friendly immune cells and match 3 or more of the same strain to wipe them out.

  • Glowing medicine gives powers: ice pack, antiviral or vaccine burst.
  • Hit in a row to build a streak bonus.
  • Tap the white blood cell to swap your immune cell.
  • Viruses mutate mid-level. Some changes help, some hurt. Watch for the warning.
