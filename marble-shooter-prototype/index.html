Scarab Coil

Marbles roll along the spiral toward the hole. Shoot marbles into the chain to make groups of 3 or more of one colour. Clear the chain before it reaches the end.

Aim, then release to shoot · tap the scarab (or right-click, or Space) to swap marbles
